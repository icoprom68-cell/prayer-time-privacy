Kohët e Namazit — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

Kohët e Namazit respects your privacy.

Location

The app uses device location only when you choose location-based features, such as finding your current location, calculating prayer times for your position, or determining Qibla direction. The app does not operate a developer-owned server for storing your location.

Maps and geocoding

The map and geocoding services used by the app can process network requests, IP address and location-related information according to their own privacy policies. The app uses these services to display maps and resolve locations.

Local data

Prayer-time data, selected location, language, corrections, notification preferences and appearance settings are stored locally on the device.

Notifications

Prayer notifications and widget updates are generated locally by the app. The app does not require an advertising or analytics account to provide these features.

Advertising and sale of data

The app does not intentionally collect personal information for advertising profiling and does not sell personal information.

Children

The app is not designed to collect personal information from children.

Developer contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact prayer.time@outlook.com.
